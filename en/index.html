Process

Workshop process

Bonding, testing, marking and packing: all four steps are done in-house. Click a photo to enlarge.

Bonding

Glass and flex cable are bonded at an enclosed station. Good units are racked separately.

Testing

Units that fail the lighting or touch test never reach packaging.

Assembly and laser marking

Flex cable, glue overflow and frame are checked under the lamp, with laser marking alongside.

Boxing and storage

Passed units go into KTK boxes and are sealed by model, one model per carton.
